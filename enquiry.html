Volunteer With Us

Knight Moves Chess Academy welcomes volunteers who would like to support our lessons, tournaments, workshops and community activities. Volunteers play a vital role in helping us reach more learners — whether that means assisting a coach during a lesson, helping organise a tournament, or simply sharing your love of the game with a new player.

Support Our Mission

As a non-profit academy, support from our community helps us provide chess opportunities and resources to learners who would otherwise not have access to them.

You can support Knight Moves by volunteering, donating chess equipment or sponsoring chess activities. Every contribution, large or small, helps us keep our programmes free and accessible to the learners who need them most.
