Welcome to Knight Moves Chess Academy

Welcome to a place where all pieces are valued and where you can become a champion. Whether you have never touched a chess piece before or you have been playing for years, Knight Moves Chess Academy is a place built for you to learn, grow, and belong.

Think. Plan. Move. Succeed.

Knight Moves Chess Academy is a non-profit chess academy dedicated to making chess accessible to everyone, regardless of age, skill level, or financial background. We believe every child and adult deserves the chance to discover what chess can teach them.

Our Mission

Our mission is to introduce people of all ages to the game of chess and provide a welcoming environment where players can learn, practise and develop their skills.

We believe chess can help develop critical thinking, problem-solving, patience, concentration and strategic decision-making.

What We Offer

Knight Moves Chess Academy offers a range of programmes suited to every stage of a player's journey, from a first introduction to the game through to competitive tournament preparation.

  • Beginner chess lessons
  • Intermediate chess training
  • Advanced chess development
  • Chess tournaments and competitions
  • Friendly chess activities
