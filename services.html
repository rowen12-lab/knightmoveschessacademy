Our Services and Programmes

Knight Moves Chess Academy offers chess programmes designed for different ages and experience levels. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced competitive player, our structured programmes are designed to meet you where you are and help you grow at your own pace.

Beginner Chess Programme

Our beginner programme is designed for players who are completely new to chess or are still learning the basics.

Students Learn:

  • How the chessboard is organised
  • How each chess piece moves
  • Basic chess rules
  • Check and checkmate
  • Basic chess tactics
  • Basic opening principles

Junior Chess Programme

The Junior Chess Programme introduces children to chess through practical games, puzzles and interactive activities.

The programme helps young players develop concentration, patience, problem-solving and strategic thinking.

Intermediate Chess Programme

This programme is designed for players who understand the fundamentals and want to develop stronger tactical and strategic skills.

  • Opening principles
  • Tactical combinations
  • Positional play
  • Strategic planning
  • Endgame techniques
  • Game analysis

Advanced Chess Programme

Our advanced programme is aimed at experienced players who want to prepare for competitive chess.

  • Advanced tactics
  • Opening preparation
  • Positional strategy
  • Endgame theory
  • Opponent analysis
  • Tournament preparation

Chess Workshops and Tournaments

We organise chess workshops and tournaments that allow players to practise their skills, gain experience and enjoy the competitive side of chess.
