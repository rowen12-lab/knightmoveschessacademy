About Knight Moves Chess Academy

Knight Moves Chess Academy is a non-profit chess academy dedicated to making chess accessible to people of all ages and skill levels.

We provide a welcoming environment where players can learn the game, improve their skills, participate in competitions and become part of a positive chess community.

Our Story

Knight Moves Chess Academy was created from a passion for chess and a desire to make the game more accessible within the community.

We believe that chess is more than a game. It can help people develop critical thinking, patience, concentration, problem-solving and decision-making skills.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a strong chess community where everyone has the opportunity to learn, participate and reach their full potential.

Our Values

  • Accessibility
  • Learning and development
  • Community
  • Excellence
  • Respect and good sportsmanship

Our Community

As a non-profit academy, we aim to support our community through chess workshops, school programmes, tournaments and educational activities.
