About Knight Moves Chess Academy

Knight Moves Chess Academy is a non-profit chess academy dedicated to making chess accessible to people of all ages and skill levels.

We provide a welcoming environment where players can learn the game, improve their skills, participate in competitions and become part of a positive chess community.

Our Story

Knight Moves Chess Academy was created from a passion for chess and a desire to make the game more accessible within the community.

We believe that chess is more than a game. It can help people develop critical thinking, patience, concentration, problem-solving and decision-making skills.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a strong chess community where everyone, regardless of age, background, or experience, has the opportunity to learn, participate, and reach their full potential. We want chess to be seen not as an exclusive or intimidating game, but as something open to anyone willing to learn — from complete beginners picking up a piece for the first time, to competitive players preparing for their next tournament.

Our Values

  • Accessibility
  • Learning and development
  • Community
  • Excellence
  • Respect and good sportsmanship

Our Community

As a non-profit academy, we aim to support our community through chess workshops, school programmes, tournaments and educational activities. We partner with local schools and community centres wherever possible, so that access to chess is never limited by a family's ability to pay for lessons.
